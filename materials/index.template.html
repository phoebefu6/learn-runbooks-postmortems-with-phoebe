Learn Runbooks & Postmortems with Phoebe

The page you read at 2 a.m., and the write-up that stops it happening again. Most teams can show you their best runbook and their best postmortem. These ten sessions are about the rest: a whole library of runbooks that drifts every time a system changes, and a year of postmortems whose actions quietly expire. Of every runbook and postmortem you have, how many would help the next responder, and how many would mislead them? Six Writer sessions are for the people who write, drill and maintain the pages and draft the write-ups. Four Leader sessions are for the people who decide what must exist, run the review, own the public postmortem and chase actions to done.

Writer track ✍️ · six sessions, hands-on in the browser

For on-call engineers, SREs, data and analytics engineers and tech writers. Inventory a library, watch it drift, drill it, write the team postmortem, read a year of postmortems as one, and score the whole kit on the docs bench. The bench's rule engine is real and runs on any runbook or postmortem you paste.
